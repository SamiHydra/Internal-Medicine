# Resident manual

Evaluating the consultants you work with, and the morning session. Read the
[User manual](USER-MANUAL.md) first for signing in and notifications.

## 1. Your pages

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** (`/academic`) | today's eligible subjects, your recent submissions, a summary of the evaluations you received |
| **Submit evaluation** (`/academic/submit`) | the consultant evaluation form |
| **History** (`/academic/history`) | everything you submitted, filterable by date and subject |
| **Morning session** (`/academic/morning`) | shown in the sidebar when you are a designated recorder; the page itself is reachable by anyone academic but only recorders can save |

## 2. Evaluating a consultant

1. Open **Submit evaluation**. The date defaults to today; change it if you
   are evaluating a previous day.
2. Choose the consultant. Only consultants you shared a placement with on
   that date are offered; if the one you expect is missing, the roster does
   not pair you that day (ask the administrator to check the duty roster or
   rotation plan).
3. Answer the six indicators (yes or no): all patients reviewed, management
   plan documented, VTE assessed, discharge discussed, medication review done,
   critical labs reviewed.
4. Choose the overall rating from 1 to 5. It is mandatory; submitting
   without it shows "Select overall rating."
5. Answer any extra questions the current form version asks, then submit.

One evaluation per consultant per day. Submitted evaluations cannot be
edited; if you made a mistake, tell the administrator, who can record a
correction on the academic side.

## 3. Your own performance

**Home** and **My performance** show what consultants recorded about you over
the window: mean overall rating, indicator compliance (the share of the ten
resident indicators marked true), the combined score (rating and compliance
weighted equally), and the indicators most often marked no. You appear in
rankings only once you have at least three evaluations.

## 4. The morning session

Sessions open automatically on the configured days (Monday, Wednesday and
Friday by default) at the configured time. If the administrator has made you
a recorder:

1. Open **Morning session** on the day.
2. Mark each expected person present or absent and on time or late against
   the session's start time.
3. Save. The roster and punctuality are frozen at that moment.

If you have not recorded it 15 minutes after the start, you receive a
reminder. A session nobody records stays pending; an administrator can
correct a recorded one. You cannot record another day's session.

## 5. Rotations and transfers

Your rotation block and ward come from the administrator's rotation plan and
the duty roster; they drive who you can evaluate and who evaluates you.
Section transfers are requested by consultants, not residents.

## 6. Quick answers

- *No subjects are offered today.* No shared placement on that date; try
  the correct date or ask the administrator.
- *I cannot save the morning session.* You are not a designated recorder, or
  the session is not today's.
- *My score looks wrong.* Scores use the field keys of the form, not the
  labels; the administrator can show you the underlying evaluations.
