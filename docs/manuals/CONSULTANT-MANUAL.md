# Consultant manual

Evaluating residents and students, recording teaching attendance, and
section transfers. Read the [User manual](USER-MANUAL.md) first for signing
in and notifications.

## 1. Your pages

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Home** (`/academic`) | today's eligible residents and students, recent submissions, a summary of the evaluations you received from residents |
| **Submit evaluation** (`/academic/submit`) | the resident evaluation form, and student weekly and final evaluations |
| **Students** (`/academic/teaching`) | today's teaching sessions on your ward and per-student attendance |
| **History** (`/academic/history`) | everything you submitted |
| **Morning session** (`/academic/morning`) | when you are a designated recorder |
| Transfer request (on Home) | request a move to another section; view and cancel your requests |

## 2. Evaluating a resident

1. **Submit evaluation**, choose the date (today by default).
2. Choose the resident. Only residents you shared a placement with on that
   date are offered.
3. Answer the ten indicators (yes or no): punctuality, preparation,
   presentation clarity, clinical reasoning, management planning,
   documentation timeliness, communication, professionalism, feedback
   responsiveness, follow-through.
4. Choose the overall rating 1 to 5 (mandatory), answer any extra questions,
   submit.

One evaluation per resident per day; submitted evaluations are immutable.

## 3. Evaluating students

**Students** lists the students placed with your ward this week from the
batch's placements. File a **weekly** evaluation during the placement and the
**final** evaluation at the end of the batch, with the batch's instrument.
The same one-per-day rule and immutability apply.

## 4. Teaching attendance

**Students** also shows today's teaching sessions on your ward (bedside,
teaching rounds, lectures, seminars) once the student representative has
recorded them as held. Tick who attended and save; you may correct it the
same day. Whether the session happened is the representative's record, not
yours.

## 5. The morning session

As a designated recorder you can record and cancel today's session exactly
as described in the [Resident manual](RESIDENT-MANUAL.md) section 4.

## 6. Requesting a transfer

1. From **Home**, open the transfer request card: choose the destination
   section and give a reason.
2. The **head of the destination section** decides; an administrator may
   decide instead.
3. If approved, the transfer takes effect at the **next rotation boundary**
   unless an administrator sets an earlier date; you are notified of the
   decision and the date.
4. You may cancel a request while it is pending.

Until the effective date you remain on your current roster; from that date
your section, ward service and evaluation pairings change automatically.

## 7. If you head a section

Transfer requests into your section appear on your review panel. Approve or
reject each with a note; the decision is recorded once with your name and
time. You cannot decide requests into other sections; an administrator can.

## 8. What consultants receive

Residents evaluate you on the six consultant indicators and an overall
rating; **Home** summarises them over the window with the same combined score
the residents see for themselves.

## 9. Quick answers

- *A resident or student is missing from the list.* The roster or placement
  does not pair you on that date; ask the administrator.
- *My transfer was approved but nothing changed.* It applies at the effective
  date, at 00:15 hospital time; check the date in the decision notice.
- *I cannot record attendance.* The session has not been recorded as held by
  the representative yet, or it is not today.
