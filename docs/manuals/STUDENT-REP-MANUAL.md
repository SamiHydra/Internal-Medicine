# Student representative manual

Logging whether the day's teaching happened. Read the [User manual](USER-MANUAL.md)
first for signing in and notifications.

## 1. Your page

You have one page, the **Activity log** (`/teaching`). It lists the teaching
sessions generated for your batch that fall within your scope: today's
sessions first, then recent days. Each session shows the activity type
(lecture, seminar, bedside, teaching round), the subgroup where relevant, the
scheduled time and its status.

## 2. Your scope

An administrator gave you a scope when they made you a representative:

| Scope | You log |
|---|---|
| Group | lectures and seminars for the whole cohort |
| Subgroup A or Subgroup B | bedside sessions and teaching rounds for that subgroup |

Sessions outside your scope are not shown and cannot be recorded by you.

## 3. Recording a session

1. Open the session.
2. Choose **Held** or **Not held**.
3. If **Not held**, a reason is required (the teacher was called away, the
   ward was closed, and so on). The record is refused without it.
4. Save. The session shows **Held** or **Not held**.

Who attended is recorded separately by the consultant who taught; you do not
record attendance or assessments.

## 4. Reminders

At 17:00 hospital time you receive a reminder in the app for any of today's
sessions still pending. Sessions are generated every night from the batch's
weekly schedule, so a missing session usually means the schedule does not
list that activity for the day; tell the administrator.

## 5. What you cannot see

Evaluations, scores, attendance figures, reports and dashboards are not part
of your role; every such page answers "This action is unauthorized." and
brings you back to the log. This is by design and is verified automatically.
